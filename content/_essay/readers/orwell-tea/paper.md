<a id="S001"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** If you look up ‘tea’ in the first cookery book that comes to hand you will probably find that it is unmentioned; or at most you will find a few lines of sketchy instructions which give no ruling on several of the most important points. This is curious, not only because tea is one of the mainstays of civilization in this country, as well as in Eire, Australia and New Zealand, but because the best manner of making it is the subject of violent disputes.

**中文:** 随手翻开一本烹饪书，查一查‘茶’，你多半会发现，书里根本没有提到它；即使提了，也不过是寥寥几行粗略的说明，对几个最要紧的问题并未作出判断。这很奇怪。茶不仅是这个国家，以及爱尔兰、澳大利亚和新西兰日常文明的一大支柱，究竟怎样才能泡出最好的茶，也一直是人们争论不休的话题。

<a id="S002"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** When I look through my own recipe for the perfect cup of tea, I find no fewer than 11 outstanding points. On perhaps two of them there would be pretty general agreement, but at least four others are acutely controversial. Here are my own 11 rules, every one of which I regard as golden:

**中文:** 当我仔细研究自己的一杯完美茶的配方时，我发现不少于 11 个要点。也许在其中两个问题上，人们会达成相当普遍的共识，但至少在其他四个问题上存在严重争议。以下是我自己的 11 条规则，每一条我都认为是黄金法则：

<a id="S003"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** First of all, one should use Indian or Ceylonese tea. China tea has virtues which are not to be despised nowadays—it is economical, and one can drink it without milk—but there is not much stimulation in it. One does not feel wiser, braver or more optimistic after drinking it. Anyone who has used that comforting phrase ‘a nice cup of tea’ invariably means Indian tea.

**中文:** 首先，应该使用印度茶或锡兰茶。中国茶有当今不可轻视的优点——经济，不加奶也能喝——但没有太多刺激。喝了它后，人并不会感到更聪明、更勇敢或更乐观。任何使用过“一杯好茶”这句令人安慰的短语的人都无一例外地指的是印度茶。

<a id="S004"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Secondly, tea should be made in small quantities—that is, in a teapot. Tea out of an urn is always tasteless, while army tea, made in a cauldron, tastes of grease and whitewash. The teapot should be made of china or earthenware. Silver or Britanniaware teapots produce inferior tea and enamel pots are worse; though curiously enough a pewter teapot (a rarity nowadays) is not so bad.

**中文:** 其次，泡茶应该少量，即用茶壶泡。瓮里的茶总是无味的，而大锅里的军茶却有油脂和粉饰的味道。茶壶应该由瓷器或陶器制成。银质或不列颠瓷器茶壶泡出的茶质量较差，而搪瓷茶壶则更差；但奇怪的是，锡制茶壶（现在很罕见）也不错。

<a id="S005"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Thirdly, the pot should be warmed beforehand. This is better done by placing it on the hob than by the usual method of swilling it out with hot water.

**中文:** 第三，锅要提前预热。最好将其放在炉灶上，而不是用热水冲洗的通常方法。

<a id="S006"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Fourthly, the tea should be strong. For a pot holding a quart, if you are going to fill it nearly to the brim, six heaped teaspoons would be about right. In a time of rationing, this is not an idea that can be realized on every day of the week, but I maintain that one strong cup of tea is better than twenty weak ones. All true tea lovers not only like their tea strong, but like it a little stronger with each year that passes—a fact which is recognized in the extra ration issued to old-age pensioners.

**中文:** 第四，茶要浓。对于一个装有一夸脱的锅，如果你要把它装满，六茶匙就足够了。在配给时代，这不是一个可以在一周中的每一天实现的想法，但我坚持认为一杯浓茶比二十杯淡茶要好。所有真正的茶爱好者不仅喜欢浓茶，而且喜欢一年比一年浓一点——这一事实在向养老金领取者发放的额外配给中得到了认可。

<a id="S007"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Fifthly, the tea should be put straight into the pot. No strainers, muslin bags or other devices to imprison the tea. In some countries teapots are fitted with little dangling baskets under the spout to catch the stray leaves, which are supposed to be harmful. Actually one can swallow tea-leaves in considerable quantities without ill effect, and if the tea is not loose in the pot it never infuses properly.

**中文:** 第五，茶叶应直接入壶。没有过滤器、棉布袋或其他装置来盛放茶叶。在一些国家，茶壶在壶嘴下方装有悬挂的小篮子，用来接住杂散的叶子，这些叶子被认为是有害的。事实上，一个人可以吞下相当多的茶叶而不会产生任何不良影响，如果茶在壶中不散，就永远无法正确泡茶。

<a id="S008"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Sixthly, one should take the teapot to the kettle and not the other way about. The water should be actually boiling at the moment of impact, which means that one should keep it on the flame while one pours. Some people add that one should only use water that has been freshly brought to the boil, but I have never noticed that it makes any difference.

**中文:** 第六，应该把茶壶拿向水壶，而不是相反。水在撞击时实际上应该沸腾，这意味着在倒水时应该将水保持在火焰上。有些人补充说只能使用刚烧开的水，但我从未注意到这有什么区别。

<a id="S009"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Seventhly, after making the tea, one should stir it, or better, give the pot a good shake, afterwards allowing the leaves to settle.

**中文:** 第七，泡完茶后，要搅拌一下，最好是用力摇晃茶壶，让茶叶沉降。

<a id="S010"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Eighthly, one should drink out of a good breakfast cup—that is, the cylindrical type of cup, not the flat, shallow type. The breakfast cup holds more, and with the other kind one’s tea is always half cold—before one has well started on it.

**中文:** 第八，应该用优质的早餐杯喝水，即圆柱形的杯子，而不是扁平的、浅的杯子。早餐杯的容量更大，而另一种茶在开始喝之前总是半冷的。

<a id="S011"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Ninthly, one should pour the cream off the milk before using it for tea. Milk that is too creamy always gives tea a sickly taste.

**中文:** 第九，在用牛奶泡茶之前，应该将牛奶中的奶油倒掉。太奶油的牛奶总是会给茶带来恶心的味道。

<a id="S012"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Tenthly, one should pour tea into the cup first. This is one of the most controversial points of all; indeed in every family in Britain there are probably two schools of thought on the subject. The milk-first school can bring forward some fairly strong arguments, but I maintain that my own argument is unanswerable. This is that, by putting the tea in first and stirring as one pours, one can exactly regulate the amount of milk whereas one is liable to put in too much milk if one does it the other way round.

**中文:** 第十，先把茶倒进杯子里。这是最有争议的一点之一；事实上，在英国的每个家庭中，关于这个问题可能都有两种思想流派。牛奶优先派可以提出一些相当有力的论点，但我坚持认为我自己的论点是无法反驳的。就是说，先放茶，边倒边搅拌，可以准确调节奶量，反之则容易放太多奶。

<a id="S013"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Lastly, tea—unless one is drinking it in the Russian style—should be drunk without sugar. I know very well that I am in a minority here. But still, how can you call yourself a true tea-lover if you destroy the flavour of your tea by putting sugar in it? It would be equally reasonable to put in pepper or salt. Tea is meant to be bitter, just as beer is meant to be bitter. If you sweeten it, you are no longer tasting the tea, you are merely tasting the sugar; you could make a very similar drink by dissolving sugar in plain hot water.

**中文:** 最后，茶——除非是以俄罗斯风格喝茶——应该不加糖喝。我很清楚我在这里是少数。但是，如果你在茶里加糖，破坏了茶的味道，你怎么能称自己是真正的爱茶人呢？放胡椒或盐同样合理。茶本来就是苦的，就像啤酒本来就是苦的一样。如果你加糖，你就不再品尝茶，而只是品尝糖；您可以通过将糖溶解在普通热水中来制作非常类似的饮料。

<a id="S014"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** Some people would answer that they don’t like tea in itself, that they only drink it in order to be warmed and stimulated, and they need sugar to take the taste away. To those misguided people I would say: Try drinking tea without sugar for, say, a fortnight and it is very unlikely that you will ever want to ruin your tea by sweetening it again.

**中文:** 有些人会回答说，他们本身不喜欢茶，喝茶只是为了温暖和刺激，需要糖来带走味道。对于那些被误导的人，我想说：尝试喝不加糖的茶，比如说两周，你就不太可能想再次加糖来毁掉你的茶。

<a id="S015"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** These are not the only controversial points to arise in connection with tea drinking, but they are sufficient to show how subtilized the whole business has become.

**中文:** 这些并不是与喝茶有关的唯一有争议的点，但它们足以表明整个行业已经变得多么微妙。

<a id="S016"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** There is also the mysterious social etiquette surrounding the teapot (why is it considered vulgar to drink out of your saucer, for instance?) and much might be written about the subsidiary uses of tealeaves, such as telling fortunes, predicting the arrival of visitors, feeding rabbits, healing burns and sweeping the carpet.

**中文:** 还有围绕茶壶的神秘社交礼仪（例如，为什么用茶碟喝水被认为是粗俗的？），并且可以写很多关于茶叶的辅助用途的文章，例如算命、预测访客的到来、喂兔子、治疗烧伤和扫地毯。

<a id="S017"></a>
**Source:** https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/a-nice-cup-of-tea/

**Original:** It is worth paying attention to such details as warming the pot and using water that is really boiling, so as to make quite sure of wringing out of one’s ration the 20 good, strong cups that two ounces, properly handled, ought to represent.

**中文:** 值得注意的是诸如加热锅和使用真正沸腾的水之类的细节，以确保从一个人的口粮中榨出20杯优质的、坚固的杯子，即2盎司，如果处理得当，应该代表20杯。
